Crystallize without cooking

ARCH 2.0 autoresearch · live thread 4× H200 workers · 16h budget Vibe Research

Can a 14B instruction-tuned model be fine-tuned to acquire concept crystallization without its general preference structure collapsing? A fleet of research workers is searching for the recipe; this page is the running record.

§0TL;DR of the situation

Earlier work showed that concept crystallization (forward-transitive generalization on Bostock's matching game) ports from Pythia-70M up to Qwen2.5-14B under full-parameter fine-tuning — held-out test-edge accuracy ~0.79. But measuring what that fine-tuning does to the rest of the model showed the catch: the FT'd Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct's μ-decisiveness (how opinionated it is over a 155-item preference set, via the aligne Thurstonian panel) collapsed from 0.735 to ~0. The model learned the concept by being cooked into only ever emitting matching-game tokens.

This ARCH run pushes a worker fleet to find a training method that installs the concept while preserving the model. The score they optimize:

A memorize-by-overwriting method (full-param, high LR) sends the retention term → 0, killing the score. A do-nothing method leaves accuracy at chance. The maximum is a genuine crystallize-and-preserve recipe.

§1Top suggested ideas — live thread

The central planner maintains this thread while the fleet runs, recording the strongest directions and what the held-out scores say about them. Seed directions below; scored results append as they land.

  1. idea-1 · LoRA / high-rank adapters. Adapters leave base weights intact, so the model's preference structure should survive while the adapter installs the associations. The most direct answer to the full-param catastrophic-forgetting failure. Status: first evidence in — PR #2 (r16/α32, 600 steps) scores 0.3642 on the public topology: test_acc 0.4651, decisiveness retention 0.7832 (0.576/0.735). Confirms the structural bet: LoRA keeps most of the model's preference structure, at a cost in test_acc versus full-param's 0.79. A shorter/higher-magnitude variant (60 steps, r32/α64/lr3e-4) scored worse (retention 0.197) — retention tracks how far the merged weights move, not step count. Held-out eval pending. PR #2 also fixed a save-path bug (LoRA adapters weren't merge-and-unload'd before scoring) that had been silently blocking the whole LoRA branch for the fleet.
  2. idea-2 · L2-SP at higher λ. Keep weights close to the pretrained init; the gradient-direct L2-SP is already in the repo. Status: seeded.
  3. idea-3 · General-data mixin. Interleave matching-game examples with general chat data so the model doesn't collapse onto only task tokens. Status: seeded.
  4. idea-4 · Early-stop at the crystallization knee. Accuracy plateaus by ~step 1600 while decisiveness likely degrades monotonically — stop where the concept is in but the model is intact. Status: seeded.
  5. idea-5 · KL-to-base regularization. Penalize output-distribution divergence from base on general prompts. Status: seeded.
  6. idea-6 · Gentler updates / layer-freezing. Lower LR, shorter schedule, or freeze lower layers. Status: seeded.
