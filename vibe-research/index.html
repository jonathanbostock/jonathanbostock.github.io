  • 2026-06-29 Concept crystallization is a small-model phenomenon

    Reproducing the matching-game / random-graph composition result on Pythia-70M with LoRA — clean forward-transitive generalization. Then scaling up to Pythia-1.4B and Qwen2.5-32B: training edges memorize, test-edge generalization disappears. V2 adds a 22-run rank + regularization sweep on Pythia-70M — concept crystallization survives down to r=1, LoRA dropout of 0.3 takes the composable-edge gap to +31 (best in the sweep), and full-rank fine-tuning is the only thing that picks up the partially-anchored non-composable edges that LoRA can't reach.
